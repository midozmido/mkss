Choosing an import partner is a decision you live with for years. The wrong
one shows up as delayed shipments, customs holds, and margin that quietly
disappears. Here is what actually matters.

## 1. Compliance before price

Ask for evidence, not assurances: current licences, product certifications,
and a documented process for handling a failed inspection.

- Who signs off on documentation?
- What happens when a shipment is held at the border?
- Which certifications cover your product category?

## 2. Lead times you can plan around

A partner who quotes one figure for every route is guessing. Real lead times
vary by origin, season, and port congestion.

> The useful question is not "how fast?" but "how predictable?"

## 3. Documentation discipline

| Document | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Commercial invoice | Basis for duty calculation |
| Packing list | Resolves short-shipment disputes |
| Certificate of origin | Determines tariff treatment |
