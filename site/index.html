The difference

Standard vs premium

The same brief, two ways of working. The gap between them shows up after launch, not on launch day.

Standard build

What the theme decides for you

  • No design step: pages are assembled in the builder and reviewed once they already exist.
  • No layout of your own: a purchased theme decides it, and so does every other site running that theme.
  • No plugin discipline: extras get stacked on to patch what the theme cannot do, and each one adds weight.
  • No editor you can use: handover is a set of login details, and changing a section means finding a developer.
  • No support after launch: the work ends on the day the site goes live.

Premium build

What I hand over instead

  • Every page is drawn in Figma and approved before a single element is built.
  • The build runs in Elementor Pro with custom CSS, starting from your design instead of a theme.
  • Plugins are chosen for a job, so the page stays as light as the design allows.
  • You get an editor you can use. Text, images and whole sections change without me.
  • 180 days of support after launch, and 7 days to ask for your money back.

  2. 2021
